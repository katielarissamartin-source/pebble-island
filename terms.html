Pebble Island prototype information

Updated 23 October 2026. This free demonstration supports family exploration and product testing. It has no paid subscriptions or school licences available for purchase.

Learning content

The lessons are original supplemental teaching and practice activities informed by Australian Curriculum Version 9.0 and Queensland Curriculum and Assessment Authority resources. They have not been certified, independently teacher-moderated or validated as a complete school program. Topic confidence indicators are estimates from brief quizzes, not diagnoses, formal results, learning disabilities assessments or school grades.

Availability and progression

Use at your discretion with appropriate adult supervision. This experimental app may be updated and errors may exist. Local progress may be lost when browser storage is cleared. Back up valuable learning records, and do not rely on the app as the only educational resource.

Pricing concepts and educator pilots

All subscription and school prices displayed in the adult area are illustrative concepts only. Clicking the pricing screen never starts a paid plan, places an order or establishes school access. The classroom view currently runs only on one device and does not connect to a school's systems.

Privacy and third parties

See our privacy and learning-data explanation for device storage, optional original game cloud services and third-party requests. Do not use real school identifiers or sensitive personal information in prototype testing.
